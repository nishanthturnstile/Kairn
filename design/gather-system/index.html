Bricolage for the story.

Source Sans 3 for the work. Body 18/27; controls 16/22; small supporting text 14/20. Mixed-script source text keeps a verified fallback.

Four curves, not one.

12 px dense items · 20 px fields · 28 px surfaces · full-pill actions. Photographs have opposite 64/20 px corners. Chapter tabs meet a shared page edge.

Small motion, clear cause.

Press 120 ms · change 180 ms · unfold 220 ms. No looping shimmer, autoplay, magnetic targets or animated consent.
