Membership identity is unique across active/left/removed states. Capability rows bind both membership and journey, record their accepted epoch, and cannot become current again after a rejoin. The latest ended participation records its previous powers before revocation; historical capability epochs remain revoked. One partial unique index plus deferred graph validation enforces one active administrator, or an explicit frozen/deleted state.
